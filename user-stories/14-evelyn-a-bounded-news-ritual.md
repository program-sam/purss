# 14 — Evelyn: a quieter everyday internet, not another project

## Fictional first-person scenario

I teach secondary school, but what I want after breakfast isn't a teaching tool.
I want the local paper, a couple of trusted national outlets, new videos from
people I enjoy, and the posts of friends who still bother writing blogs. I also
like gardening, ridiculous film commentary, and a niche creator who restores
old maps. None of it needs to be turned into self-improvement.

I read on a tablet at the kitchen table and use my phone on the bus. At the moment
I open Substack for a writer, another app for a video, and a browser for the news.
Then the recommendations take over and my twenty-minute break becomes an hour.
I'd like to see the things I actually came for and have a natural point to stop.

Some days I want the serious news; other days I want the map restoration and a
friend's holiday diary. A good article might become useful for a lesson next term,
but most reading is just reading. If I miss a week, I don't want to spend my
next free morning sorting everything I didn't get around to. Sometimes I'd like
to skip a topic for a month without unfollowing a writer I otherwise enjoy.
I'd pay a modest amount for something calm that I actually kept using.

## What matters to me

- Enjoy an ordinary mixture without an attention-grabbing system steering it.
- Choose the mood of a visit without rebuilding my sources.
- Keep a few lasting discoveries and let the rest remain uncomplicated.
