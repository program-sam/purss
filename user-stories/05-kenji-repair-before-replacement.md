# 05 — Kenji: repairs, football, friends, and things I watch for fun

## Fictional first-person scenario

I volunteer at a repair cafe in Rotterdam, so I follow a few careful repairers
on YouTube and an electronics forum. But on a normal evening I'm just as likely
to watch football analysis, read a webcomic, or swap cycling links with a friend
on Signal. There's a tiny creator making stop-motion films from broken appliances
whom I follow purely because the films make me laugh.

I browse on a desktop at home and my phone during the day. Something from the
fun part of my browsing occasionally becomes useful at the repair cafe; usually
it doesn't, and that's fine. I want to hear from the people I picked without
an appliance video persuading a platform that I now want to buy appliances.

Saturday is different. With a battered tablet on the workbench and people waiting,
I need the exact reference for a particular sewing machine, not all the things I
watched that week. Advice about dangerous electrical work needs checking against
the original source. I'd like the casual discoveries and the checked references
to coexist. At the cafe, my hands are often dirty and someone else needs the
tablet next; finding the right model number in seconds makes a real difference.

## What matters to me

- Enjoy a mixed set of creators without recommendation drift.
- Find a specific checked reference quickly when a repair actually needs it.
- Keep a friend's cycling links from vanishing in chat.
