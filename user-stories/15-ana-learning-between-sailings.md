# 15 — Ana: my interests ashore and between sailings

## Fictional first-person scenario

I work on a ferry. At home I watch YouTube, read the news, catch up on friends'
voice messages, and follow a few small comedy and craft creators. I'm learning
furniture restoration, but that isn't my entire online life. On a long shift break I might
prefer an interview or a football podcast to another lesson about varnish.

My phone and small tablet move between decent Wi-Fi and very unreliable internet.
Before leaving home I want to pick a modest amount of reading or listening for
the trip. I need to know what will really be available, not discover halfway
through a quiet evening that everything I remembered is just an unreachable link.
I've opened a saved video at sea before and found only its title. That's a long break.

Once ashore again, my downloads and remembered links are scattered across apps.
Sometimes a restoration idea is worth keeping for a weekend project; a funny clip
usually isn't. Storage is tight, and I already
use some services' own download options. I'd rather have an honest combination
of things that work than a promise that every website behaves the same way.

## What matters to me

- Bring a varied slice of my everyday interests through changing connectivity.
- Know what's genuinely available away from the network.
- Catch up selectively instead of downloading or processing everything.
