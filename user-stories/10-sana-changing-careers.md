# 10 — Sana: a career change that doesn't swallow my evenings

## Fictional first-person scenario

I'm moving from hospitality into building conservation. My phone now has lots of
employer pages, courses, and trade notices open, but I also use it for comedy,
recipes, local news, and friends' updates. I follow a furniture restorer mostly
on Instagram and someone on Twitch who talks about old games. Only one relates to
the career change; both are people I actually want to hear from.

On breaks I glance at whatever catches my interest. An ordinary evening might begin
with a cooking video and end with an apprenticeship that looks promising. I'd
like to come back to both for different reasons without searching my messages,
watch history, and bookmarks separately.

At home I share a laptop that gets cleared regularly, and I lose promising
opportunities. When I find them again, I need to remember why one was unsuitable
and whether another is still open. I already have accounts with some job sites;
I don't want to hand over those logins or my identity documents just to remember
a vacancy.

## What matters to me

- Keep exploration and entertainment alongside practical plans.
- Resume an interesting thread even when I change devices.
- Give urgent opportunities attention without making all online time feel urgent.
