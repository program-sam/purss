# 17 — Bea: studio references and whatever catches my eye

## Fictional first-person scenario

I make costumes for small theatre productions, but plenty of my browsing has no
brief attached. I watch comedy and home-renovation videos, swap links with friends
in a group chat, and keep up with a few illustrators and craftspeople. Museums,
textile-conservation pages, and historical fashion channels sit among those interests,
not in a completely separate professional world.

On the train I use a phone; in the studio I have a large screen. A color combination
from a music video may matter for a costume later. A craftsperson's post can be
inspiring without becoming a reference I have to organize. Sometimes I want to
browse visually; sometimes I'm looking for one sleeve shape and its original maker.

The trouble is moving between those intentions. My current folders contain
screenshots called things like "blue dress final 2," and my watch history contains
nearly everything else. I'd like to enjoy casual discovery while still being able
to return to a small selection with attribution intact. A reminder and a link
are often enough; sometimes I need a modest copy for a rehearsal without reception.

## What matters to me

- Recognize people and images across a mixture of work and leisure.
- Turn a casual discovery into a traceable reference when I choose.
- Find the maker to ask about using an image, rather than another anonymous screenshot.
