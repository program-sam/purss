# People and situations

Twenty fictional, exploratory narratives for independent product-fit review. These
are hypotheses, not interviews, endorsements, market validation, verified feed
availability, or claims that the proposed product supports the situations.

The stories deliberately describe people's lives and desired outcomes rather than
our screens, architecture, vocabulary, or implementation. Some needs may be a poor
fit, require another service, or belong outside the product. Preserve those findings
instead of rewriting the people to match the plan. Named people are fictional;
source types/platform examples do not guarantee an available RSS feed or permission
to collect private material. No story is a feature commitment.

These mixed online lives include different combinations of entertainment, news,
friends, small creators, and interests or responsibilities that need more care.
Review the combination, not just the niche. Combining these sources need not mean
putting everything in one indiscriminate stream, or moving every online activity
into PURSS. Leisure is a valid outcome without organizing or saving it.
