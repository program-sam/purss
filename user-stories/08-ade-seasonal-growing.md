# 08 — Ade: a growing season and a life outside the garden

## Fictional first-person scenario

I run a small market garden and follow extension notices, growers' blogs, soil
research explainers, and weather discussions. I also keep up with a football
podcast, national news, a cousin's photo newsletter, and a couple of video channels
about old motorcycles. I don't stop being interested in those things when the
season gets busy; I just have less patience for checking five different places.

In winter I read on the shared laptop. During the season it's usually an Android
phone, sometimes with patchy reception. At lunch I might want a short football
piece rather than more growing advice. On a rainy evening I might be ready to
learn about irrigation or watch someone restore a motorcycle carburetor.

Useful material can matter long after publication. I want to find that irrigation
explanation when I need it and a travel suggestion when we finally plan a break.
My partner uses the same laptop, so leaving a dozen tabs open doesn't help. For
weather warnings I still use the official service directly; a missed frost alert
can cost me a crop, unlike an interesting article I get to next week.

## What matters to me

- Choose what kind of attention I have time for, not only what's newest.
- Keep up with ordinary interests as well as a seasonal occupation.
- Use little data and understand when practical information needs a fresh check.
