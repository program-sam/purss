# 11 — Ivo: a volunteer responsibility among everyday interests

## Fictional first-person scenario

Most evenings on my Linux laptop start with city news, a chess channel, a few
friends' posts in a small Discord server, or someone teaching fermentation. I also maintain a small open-source
tool in my spare time. That means following dependency releases, maintainers'
posts, and technical discussions. I enjoy sorting things out, including making
lists of chess openings to practice; a little organization is part of the fun.

I often notice things on my phone and return after dinner. Sometimes I've got
the energy to understand a software change; sometimes I want to watch a chess
match or catch up on the Discord conversation. I don't want to rebuild the
same set of sources across a developer tool and several leisure services.

Technical items need precise versions and original release notes when I finally
work on them. I already have an issue tracker and direct security alerts. A
recipe or match recap doesn't need to be treated like another issue to close.
What would help is remembering what I chose to follow and where I left off,
without handing a new service access to the private Discord conversations.

## What matters to me

- Keep volunteer work available without having it dominate leisure.
- Preserve exact technical context when it matters and stay casual when it doesn't.
- Bring a mixed set of chosen sources together with modest maintenance effort.
