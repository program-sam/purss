# 13 — Luca: culture, friends, and the occasional grant deadline

## Fictional first-person scenario

I volunteer with a neighborhood theatre group in Hamburg. Outside that I follow
music channels, a small comic artist, local news, and friends who post about art,
food, and whatever they're making. Some of the best things I find are neither
useful nor important; they're just why I wanted to be online that evening.

The theatre adds another layer: rehearsal-space notices, small grants, training,
and arts organizations' announcements. A video about lighting could be relevant
to a production or just a fascinating watch. An artist's personal post might lead
to an exhibition I'd enjoy with friends. I don't want all of that divided by
which website it came from. Last month I remembered an exhibition only after
it closed; the grant deadline was easy to find, but the enjoyable outing wasn't.

I browse on my phone during commutes and a laptop at the rehearsal space. Once
in a while I need a focused session checking deadlines and eligibility. Then I
want to leave those tasks behind and return to ordinary browsing. The group has
other tools for shared documents; I mostly need a personal place to remember,
with a way to send a few links without exposing everything else I read.

## What matters to me

- Keep everyday culture and a volunteer commitment connected but not compulsory.
- Remember events in time to go, as well as deadlines we might apply for.
- Pass along chosen references while keeping the rest of my interests private.
