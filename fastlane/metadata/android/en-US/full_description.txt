PURSS is a calm, private place for everything you follow: blogs, news sites, YouTube channels, podcasts, newsletters, Mastodon and Bluesky. You decide what comes in and in what order. There's no algorithm, no ads, and no AI you didn't ask for.

• Home shows what's new from your sources, grouped the way you like.
• Keep anything in your own baskets: it stays even when the original page changes or disappears.
• Read articles, listen to podcasts and watch videos in one place, with your progress remembered.
• Save items for offline reading on this phone.
• Search everything you follow and keep.

The app connects to your PURSS: the hosted service at purss.app (free plan available) or a PURSS you host yourself. Your library lives there; the app keeps downloads and settings on this device.

This build has no Google services, so it doesn't receive push notifications; notifications inside the app work as usual.

Open source (AGPL-3.0): https://github.com/program-sam/purss
