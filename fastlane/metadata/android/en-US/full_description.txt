PURSS keeps the things you follow in one place. Connect to a PURSS server to read articles, listen to podcasts, and keep items in baskets for later.

Follow feeds from FreshRSS and use search, filters, and reading progress to find what matters. Save items to collections, work through what is new, and keep an offline copy on this device when you need one.

PURSS is a client for your own PURSS server. You need an account and server address to use it. Your library and account live on that server; the app keeps downloads and settings on this device.

The F-Droid build does not receive phone push notifications. Notifications inside the app remain available.
